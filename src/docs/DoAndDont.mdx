# Do and don't

The [principles](?path=/docs/getting-started--docs) as checks for a screen.

Use it when you review a page, a component or a story. Each line comes from a principle; nothing here adds a new rule.

<div className="ds-dd">
  <div>
    <p className="ds-dd__h">Do</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Use the accent for a measured result or an interactive state.</li>
      <li>Lead each case with its reported figure, then the process.</li>
      <li>Divide sections with ruled lines; step inset surfaces one tone off the page.</li>
      <li>Make the inner radius the outer radius minus the gap between them.</li>
      <li>Animate transform and opacity only; under reduced motion, show the end state at once.</li>
      <li>Draw glyphs from the real values, and title diagrams as illustrative.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <p className="ds-dd__h">Don't</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Fill a large area with the accent, or use it for emphasis in headings (use the secondary ink).</li>
      <li>Put a figure on the page that is not in the owner's confirmed facts.</li>
      <li>Add card shadows. The consent card is the one exception.</li>
      <li>Use italics for emphasis.</li>
      <li>Animate width, height, padding or margin.</li>
      <li>Draw a glyph or diagram that suggests a value nobody measured.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
